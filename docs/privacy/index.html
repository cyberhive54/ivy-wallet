Ivy Expand — Ivy Wallet fork

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-05 · Version 1.0

About this app

This app is an offline-first personal finance workspace (budgets, a lending ledger, group expense splits). Everything you enter is stored only on your device. The app is open-source under GNU GPLv3 — the fork lives at github.com/cyberhive54/ivy-wallet.

What data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no ad networks, no crash-reporting service run by us, and no account registration of any kind. Nothing leaves your device unless you explicitly share it (e.g., sending a statement via the system share sheet).

How data is stored

  • Transactions, accounts, categories, loans, groups — local SQLite database (Room).
  • Backup files — written to whatever folder you pick (cloud drive / photos / local storage). Your file, not ours.
  • Receipts — stored in app-private storage; never uploaded.

Sharing

The only outbound actions the app initiates are the ones you tap:

  • Contact picker — system contact list; the app never reads your address book; nothing is transmitted back, nothing stored beyond what you choose to type.
  • ACTION_SEND (WhatsApp / SMS / any share sheet) — message opens pre-filled; you hit Send yourself.
  • tel: — dialler intent for calling a customer; standard Android behaviour.

Third parties

None. No Firebase. No advertising SDKs. No tracking pixels. No cookies — this is a native app. No syncing servers operated by us.

Your rights

  • Your data sits on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes what you did not export/back up.
  • Source code is public on GitHub — every behaviour claimed above can be independently verified.

Changes to this policy

Any change to this statement ships with the source code in the same repository, as commits visible in git history — no silent updates, no shadow policies.

Contact

Privacy questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/cyberhive54/ivy-wallet/issues.
